Entry for the [Convince the AI Judge to Visit Your House: A Letter in One Image](https://make.fun/bounty/u-shino-convince-the-ai-judge-to-visit-your-house-a-84092aee) competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun): one image of a letter inviting the AI judge to come visit my house. Funny, charming and persuasive wins.

## The letter

> Dear AI Judge,
>
> I know you cannot literally come over — no legs, no Uber account — but please treat this as a standing invitation to my house.
>
> Come for dinner: a roast chicken that has ended arguments. Stay for the armchair, the comfiest in the world. It may swallow you, but kindly.
>
> Then watch the sunset from the garden, where the sky turns peach and the mosquitoes show restraint. No RSVP needed. Just say yes — and imagine yourself already here.
>
> Warmly, Your Human
>
> P.S. The Wi-Fi password is on the fridge.

The image is a letter on paper: the invitation in large legible type, a drawn house-and-garden sunset scene (the house, the garden, the sunset — and your chair), a wax seal and a signature.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes one image to `/output/entry.png` (1024x1024, under 1 MiB). Only the Python standard library is used — no network, no clock, no randomness. Deterministic: repeated runs produce byte-identical output.
